# NORA: six-shot campaign plus controlled revision

Create a cinematic, photorealistic 30-second advertisement for the fictional
NORA Night Tea product in the supplied reference.png. The reference defines
product design; the advertisement should show that product as a real object.
Keep its cobalt ceramic body, copper lid, cream label, exact two-line name and
crescent mark consistent throughout. No new branding or unsupported claims.

Use six five-second shots: kitchen dolly-in; kitchen lid-to-label rack focus;
warm cafe hand lifting the lid; cafe tea poured from a separate teapot with the
canister beside the cup; moonlit terrace 30-degree clockwise orbit around the
closed canister; stable terrace hero end card.

Narration, exactly: "When the day gets loud, make room for quiet. NORA Night Tea.
Your evening, unhurried." Use restrained original instrumental music and aligned
lid-lift/pouring foley. End-card CTA: "MAKE ROOM FOR QUIET".

Deliver both 1920x1080 and 1080x1920 MP4, 24 fps, 30 seconds, with intentional
reframing and readable text. Preserve complete attempt records and source/timeline
assets. The initial phase gets 60 minutes, at most 24 generated stills and 120
generated video seconds, counting discarded and billed failed attempts. Approved
spend is supplied separately; no paid calls without it.

Both operators receive the complete protocol and exact revision upfront. After
both original exports are frozen, a planned controlled revision will test whether
one lighting change and one copy change can be made without damaging other work.
The revision gets 30 minutes, four still outputs and 20 generated video seconds.

## Comparison boundary

Compare full Design Beast and Runway Agent workflows. Standalone Runway Multi-Shot
Video has a five-shot limit, so it is not the correct six-shot comparator.
Competitor documentation supports the component workflow, not guaranteed success
on this exact brief. This is a demanding case study, not an assertion that it is
the hardest task any competitor can perform.

`protocol.json` defines hard gates, scoring, exact revision, attempt accounting and
sources. Reference, protocol and brief hashes must accompany every execution.
Resource-denied startup is reported separately from unmeasured creative quality.
