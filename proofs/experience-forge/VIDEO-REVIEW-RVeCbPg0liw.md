## Independent source check

The official `pbakaus/impeccable` repository currently documents one skill, 23
commands, 59 deterministic detector rules, live browser iteration, Codex
support, provider-native hooks, and an Apache-2.0 license. This supports the
existence and interface claims, not the video's superlative “#1” or any quality
gain on Beast's tasks.

## Decision

Do not install or activate Impeccable inside Design Beast merely from this
video. It overlaps Beast's existing design system and quality loop. First run a
matched baseline/candidate design task through the new skill-fitness gate and
measure hard-gate quality, regressions, calls, time, and human preference. The
Live accept/reject stream is additionally useful prior art for
OPP-20260804-05's governed preference exhaust.

## Claim boundary

This is verified visual/source review of the video's demonstrated interface.
It is not proof that Impeccable improves Beast, that its “worlds” are superior,
or that its output should be admitted as a Beast Pack.
