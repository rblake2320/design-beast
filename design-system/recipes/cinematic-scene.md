# Recipe — Cinematic scene / concept art

**Use for:** hero art, key art, environment concepts, mood pieces.
**Tool:** `higgsfield-generate` skill → GPT Image 2 (text-heavy/design) or Soul Cinema (people).

## Prompt skeleton
```
[SUBJECT: place/character + 2-3 concrete details],
[COMPOSITION: 24mm low-angle | 85mm portrait | wide establishing, foreground element for depth],
[LIGHTING: golden hour rim light | single practical source | overcast soft],
[ATMOSPHERE: rain, haze, dust motes, volumetric shafts],
[GRADE: teal-orange | bleach bypass | Kodak Portra | monochrome+one accent],
[ANCHOR: "in the style of Roger Deakins cinematography" | "Blade Runner 2049" | "Studio Ghibli background"],
no text, no watermark, no extra limbs
```

## Vary across the 4 candidates
Lighting direction OR camera height. Keep everything else fixed.

## Quality gates (judge against these)
- [ ] One clear focal point; eye lands there in <1s
- [ ] Light has a consistent direction (shadows agree)
- [ ] No AI sheen: skin/materials have texture, highlights aren't blown everywhere
- [ ] Edges of frame are composed, not accidental
- [ ] Atmosphere/depth: fore/mid/background separation

## Refine pass
Nano Banana ref edit on the winner: fix weak region, or re-angle while keeping identity.
Then upscale + grade.
