# Recipe — Product hero / brand shot

**Use for:** product pages, ads, Pinterest pins, banners, marketplace listings.
**Tool:** DO NOT freehand. `higgsfield-product-photoshoot` skill (modes: product_shot,
lifestyle_scene, hero_banner, closeup_product_with_person, conceptual_product, restyle) —
its backend assembles the prompt. Marketplace listings → `higgsfield-marketplace-cards`.

## Your job is only the brief
- Product: what it is + material/finish details
- Mode: pick from the list above
- Scene: surface, backdrop, props (max 2 — clutter kills product shots)
- Light: softbox studio | window light | dramatic single-source
- Where it ships: aspect ratio + safe zones for text overlay

## Consistency across a set
Generate ONE hero you love → use **restyle mode** for every variant (seasonal, colorway,
lifestyle). Never regenerate the product from scratch per shot — that's how sets drift.

## Quality gates
- [ ] Product is sharpest thing in frame; label/logo legible and undistorted
- [ ] Reflections/shadows physically plausible on the surface it sits on
- [ ] Background supports, never competes (blur or clean gradient)
- [ ] Negative space where the headline will go (if ad/banner)

## Refine pass
Winner → Nano Banana edit for label fixes / prop removal → upscale → grade to brand palette.
